WHAT IS THE BLACKPILL?

The Blackpill is a philosophy that recognizes the harsh realities of sexual selection in modern society. It acknowledges that physical appearance, particularly facial attractiveness, height, and other genetic traits, are the primary determinants of romantic and sexual success.

KEY PRINCIPLES OF THE BLACKPILL:

  • Physical Attractiveness is Paramount: Looks are the single most important factor in dating success, surpassing personality, wealth, or status.
  • The 80/20 Rule: Approximately 80% of women pursue the top 20% of men, creating extreme competition among the remaining 80% of men.
  • Hypergamy: Women naturally seek partners of higher status than themselves, leading to a hierarchical mating market.
  • Lookism: Society discriminates based on physical appearance, with attractive people receiving preferential treatment in all aspects of life.
  • Genetic Determinism: Physical traits that determine attractiveness are largely genetic and cannot be significantly improved through effort.

THE PILL METAPHOR

The term "Blackpill" comes from the Matrix movie metaphor:

  • Bluepill: Living in ignorance of dating realities, believing in just-world fallacies like "personality matters most."
  • Redpill: Partial awareness of dating dynamics, often focusing on game and self-improvement while still believing effort can overcome looks disadvantages.
  • Blackpill: Full acceptance that physical appearance is the primary determinant of dating success and that unattractive men face insurmountable odds.

LOOKS THEORY 101

Looks Theory is the scientific study of what makes faces physically attractive. Research has identified specific facial features and proportions that are universally considered attractive across cultures.

KEY FACIAL FEATURES

  • Jawline: A strong, defined jawline is one of the most important facial features for male attractiveness.
  • Canthal Tilt: Positive canthal tilt (outer eye corner higher than inner) is associated with youth and attractiveness.^1^
  • Forward Growth: Proper maxillary and mandibular forward projection creates harmony and attractiveness.
  • Chin: A prominent, well-defined chin is crucial for male facial attractiveness.^2^
  • Bone Structure: Strong cheekbones and brow ridge contribute to masculine appearance.
  • Facial Harmony: The overall balance and proportion of facial features matters more than any single feature.^3^

FACIAL RATIOS AND PROPORTIONS

Mathematical ratios play a crucial role in perceived attractiveness:

  • Golden Ratio: Facial proportions that approximate the golden ratio (1:1.618) are perceived as most attractive.
  • Facial Thirds: An attractive face is divided into equal thirds: hairline to eyebrows, eyebrows to nose tip, nose tip to chin.
  • Facial Width-to-Height Ratio (fWHR):strong> Lower fWHR is associated with trustworthiness and attractiveness in men.
  • Midface Ratio: The distance between pupils and mouth affects perceived attractiveness.

HEIGHT AND BODY PROPORTIONS

Beyond facial features, overall physical appearance matters greatly:

  • Height: Taller men are consistently rated as more attractive, with 6'0" to 6'2" being ideal in most Western cultures.
  • Shoulder-to-Waist Ratio: A V-shaped torso with broad shoulders and narrow waist is universally attractive in men.
  • Frame: Bone structure and skeletal frame contribute significantly to perceived attractiveness.
  • Hair: Full hair with good hairline and density is important for male attractiveness.

THE HALO EFFECT

The Halo Effect explains how attractive people receive advantages in all aspects of life:

  • Attractive individuals are perceived as more intelligent, competent, and trustworthy
  • They receive preferential treatment in hiring, promotions, and social interactions
  • The legal system shows bias toward attractive defendants
  • Attractive children receive more attention and positive reinforcement

THE 80/20 RULE EXPLAINED

The 80/20 Rule, also known as the Pareto Principle in the context of dating, states that approximately 80% of women pursue only the top 20% of men. This creates extreme competition among the remaining 80% of men for female attention.

EVIDENCE FOR THE 80/20 RULE

  • Dating App Statistics: Data from Tinder, OKCupid, and other platforms show extreme inequality in match distribution
  • Female Mate Selection: Women are naturally more selective due to higher parental investment in offspring
  • Hypergamy: Women tend to "marry up" or date partners of higher perceived status
  • Social Media Validation: Attractive men receive disproportionate attention and validation online

CONSEQUENCES OF THE 80/20 RULE

  • Increased competition among average men for female attention
  • Development of "incel" communities among men excluded from dating
  • Rise of "looksmaxxing" culture focused on physical improvement
  • Psychological impact of rejection on self-esteem and mental health

The "heightpill" is the realization that height significantly impacts dating success:

SUB-8 THEORY EXPLAINED

Sub-8 Theory posits that men rated below 8/10 in attractiveness face significant disadvantages in the dating market, with only men rated 8 or above experiencing consistent success.

  • 5-6/10: Average - faces inconsistent results and high rejection rates
  • 7/10: Above average - experiences moderate success but still faces rejection
  • 8-10/10: Attractive - experiences consistent dating success with minimal effort

EVIDENCE FOR SUB-8 THEORY

  • Dating App Data: Only men in the top 20% of attractiveness receive consistent matches
  • Cold Approach Studies: Men rated below 8/10 face extremely high rejection rates
  • Female Standards: Women consistently rate most men as below average in attractiveness
